How to Prepare for a Trip

1. Pack Smart

Take comfortable clothes, important documents, chargers and basic medicine.

2. Plan Your Route

Check the places you want to visit and calculate how much time you need.

3. Try Local Food

Local food is a simple way to experience the culture of a new country.
